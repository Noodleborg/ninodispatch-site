On the job

What happens when you can't pick up

This is for the calls that arrive while you are under a sink, on a roof, driving, or already on the phone. It uses your carrier's call forwarding. There is no new phone system to learn.

Your phone can still ring first

Most people choose missed calls only. Your line rings. If you don't answer, you're on another call, or the phone is off, the AI picks up. You can instead send every call straight to the AI.

Callers hear your business

The greeting uses your business name. You can write it, or we write one for you to approve. We recommend it says it is your business's AI assistant. The receptionist speaks English only.

It collects the details you choose

You decide what to ask, such as name, callback number, address, and what the caller needs. It can also answer simple questions from the hours and services you give us. It won't quote a price unless you tell us which prices are OK to share.

You get a summary

After the call, we can text your mobile and email a summary to up to 3 addresses: who called, how to reach them, and what they needed. You call them back yourself.

It can decline work you don't do

Tell us what you don't offer and what it should never promise. It stays on that script and can politely say no.

Nights and weekends, if calls forward

When a call is forwarded at night, on a weekend, or on a holiday, the receptionist answers it. You tell us whether to mention an after-hours rate, and whether to name the amount or only say a different rate applies.

We build it, then you forward

We set up a receptionist number with your greeting and test it by calling it ourselves. Then we email forwarding steps for your carrier. Turning forwarding on takes about 2 minutes on your phone. You confirm it by calling your business number from another phone.

Forwarding steps on the site cover T-Mobile, Verizon, and AT&T mobile lines, on iPhone and Android. For a landline or VoIP line, say which provider in the form and we work out the steps. Prepaid lines can differ, so check with your carrier. Forwarded calls may use your plan's minutes or be billed by the carrier.

Before you rely on it

What this service does not do

Nino Dispatch is not an emergency service. It must not be used for 911 or other emergency calls. Anyone with an emergency should call 911. Answering a forwarded call is not the same as sending a technician.

You can tell us what you consider urgent, a number to transfer those calls to, the hours a transfer is allowed, and what to do if the transfer does not connect. If you don't want transfers, it takes a message instead. A transfer goes to a number you already use. We do not roll a truck.

If you want the receptionist to book appointments, say so in setup and tell us your calendar and rules. We confirm how booking will work before you rely on it. If you don't want that, choose message-taking only. The caller details still come to you, and you decide what to schedule.

The AI can misunderstand a caller or get something wrong, and a forwarded call can fail if your carrier or a provider we use has an outage. We don't guarantee that every call will be answered, or that you will book more work. For calls that do forward, the AI replaces your carrier voicemail.

Nino Dispatch is remote only. We set this up for contractors in the Houston area and elsewhere, but there is no shop, office, or service van to visit, and we don't publish a personal phone number or a home address. Your service area is the one you give us. Callers still dial your existing business number. Questions go to michael@ninodispatch.com. Call (832) 737-0263 to hear our AI receptionist answer live.

Call recordings, transcripts, and caller details may be stored and are processed by the third-party providers that run the receptionist. We use that information to deliver your messages and run the service, and we don't sell it. Details are in the privacy policy and the terms.

FAQ

Practical questions

Anything else, email michael@ninodispatch.com. Or call (832) 737-0263 to hear our AI receptionist answer live.

Does my phone still ring while I'm on a job?

If you choose missed calls only, yes. Your phone rings first. The AI answers only when you don't, you're already on a call, or the phone is off or unreachable. If you choose all calls, your phone does not ring and every call goes to the AI. You can turn forwarding off later.

Do I keep my business number?

Yes. Callers keep dialing the number you already use. Forwarding sends the calls you choose to the receptionist number we set up. For those forwarded calls, the AI replaces your carrier voicemail.

Do you have an office in Houston?

No. Nino Dispatch is remote only. We can set the receptionist up for a Houston-area contractor, or for a contractor somewhere else, but there is no local office, no home address on this site, and no personal phone number to call. You can call (832) 737-0263 to hear our AI receptionist answer live. That number is answered by the AI, not a person. Email is how we work with you.

Will someone from Nino Dispatch show up at the job?

No. The service answers the phone and sends you the summary. You, or your own crew, decide whether to call the person back or take the work. We do not dispatch a technician.

What about after-hours calls?

If the call forwards, the receptionist answers it at night, on weekends, and on holidays. During setup you can say whether after-hours, weekend, or holiday visits cost more, and whether the AI should give that price or only mention that a different rate applies. That is a message to the caller. It is not an after-hours dispatch service.

Can it book the appointment for me?

Only if you ask for that. The setup form has a choice: just take messages, or book appointments. Booking needs your calendar and your rules, and we confirm how it will work during setup. If you're not sure, start with messages. You still get the caller's details.

How do I try it without signing up?

Use Try the receptionist. It opens the Demo Plumbing & Air demo in your browser. Allow the microphone. The call is limited to 3 minutes, and you don't need to share real customer information. You can also play the sample recording on this page.

What if the AI is wrong?

If it doesn't know the answer, it takes a message so you can call back, or it follows the instructions you gave. You can also list things it should never say. It can still make mistakes. Read the limits above, and the terms, before you count on it.
